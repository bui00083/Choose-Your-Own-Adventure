##Inviting a Friend


You are still sitting in the classroom after everyone else has gone.

The hallway is quiet. Most lights are off, and the soft clicking of your keyboard is the only sound. 

Then, footsteps echo outside the door. You look up.

A shadowy figure stands at the classroom doorway. You can't tell if it's a person—or something else.

Before you can speak, your phone vibrates. A message from the same figure flashes on the screen:

“Hey, did you finish your part of the group project yet?”

You glance back at the door. The figure hasn't moved.

What do you do next?
