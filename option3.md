## After Hours

You take a deep breath and reply to your teammate's message.

"Yes, I finished my part. Are you still around?"

For a moment, the classroom feels calm. The golden glow of the sunset streams through the windows, painting the room in warm light. You hear the faint hum of the heater and the soft tapping of your own fingers on the keyboard. A small smile spreads across your face—everything seems peaceful.

Then, a distant thump echoes from the hallway. Your head snaps toward the door.

Someone is walking by—slow steps, almost deliberate. You can't tell who it is. The footsteps pause right outside your door, then move away. A faint scratching sound follows, like something brushing against the wall.

You glance at the window. At first, you think it’s just a trick of the light. But a shadow flits past the glass. Someone—or something—is watching you from outside. Their face is obscured, but their gaze feels heavy, piercing. You feel your pulse quicken.

Another sound comes from somewhere deeper in the school—a low, almost human whisper. You can’t make out the words, but the tone sends shivers down your spine.

Your phone vibrates violently. A message flashes:

"They see you."

You swallow hard. The warmth from earlier is gone, replaced by a gnawing dread. Every shadow feels alive. Every distant sound feels close. You realize you are truly alone now.

What do you do next?
