# After Class

You are still sitting in the classroom after class has ended.

A few hours ago, your team stayed behind to discuss the group project. After the discussion, everyone packed their bags, said goodbye, and went home. You told yourself you would only stay for a little longer to finish some work before leaving too.

But one hour passes.

The hallway outside is quiet now. Most of the lights are off, and the only sound is the soft clicking of your keyboard.

Then, you hear footsteps.

You look up.

One of your teammates is standing in front of the classroom door.

Before you can say anything, your phone lights up.

It is a message from that same teammate:

“Hey, did you finish your part of the group project yet?”

You look back at the door.

Your teammate is still standing there.
