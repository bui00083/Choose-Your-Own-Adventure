# Walk to the door


You slowly stand up from your chair.

The classroom suddenly feels colder.

Your teammate is still standing at the doorway, motionless.

The hallway lights flicker once.

Then twice.

You carefully walk toward the door.

“Hey... are you okay?” you ask quietly.

No response.

Suddenly, the projector screen flashes brightly.

The classroom lights buzz overhead.

A line of text slowly appears on the screen.

“DON’T LET THEM IN.”

You freeze.

The figure standing at the doorway slowly tilts its head.

Then the classroom lights shut off completely.

Everything goes dark.

The last thing you hear is footsteps entering the room.
